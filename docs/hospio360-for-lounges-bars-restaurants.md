# Hospio360 for lounges, bars and restaurants: what's different

One page for the sales and onboarding team. A lounge, bar, restaurant or club runs on the same Hospio360 as a hotel, without
anything about rooms. Full detail: hospio-backend `docs/plan-venue-type.md`; how staff work: `docs/restaurant-bar.md`.

## In one line

Same product, same price (a share of daily sales on one monthly invoice), but the screens, emails and messages say **lounge**,
**bar** or **restaurant**, and everything about rooms, bookings and stays is simply not there.

## What they get

| Area | A lounge, bar or restaurant | A hotel (unchanged) |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-up / onboarding | "What kind of business?" Lounge, Bar, Restaurant, Hotel or Mixed. Step 3 is **Outlets** (bar, restaurant, kitchen), not Rooms & prices. No check-in times | Rooms & prices, check-in and check-out times |
| Menu | Dashboard, Staff, Financials, Payments, Night audit, Menus, Inventory, Reports, Approvals, **Lounge setup** (or Bar / Restaurant setup) | plus Rooms, Bookings, Folios, Organizations, Front office, Housekeeping; **Hotel setup** |
| Owner | **Locations** page ("Add a location" asks the kind), "your locations" | **Hotels** page ("Add new hotel"), "your hotels" |
| Roles | Manager, Bar, Kitchen, **Waiter**, **Cashier**, Inventory, Finance (shared tablets with PIN tiles) | all of those plus Front office, Housekeeping, Laundry, Maintenance |
| Selling | Tabs by table or guest name, kitchen and bar tickets, split the bill (equally, by item, by amount), discounts and complimentary items with a reason and a limit, void and discount requests approved by the manager, optional tips | the same in its restaurant and bar, plus **Charge to room** |
| Money | Cash into the till of whoever takes it (a waiter's into the cashier's), card and transfer on the outlet's POS, tills counted and handed over | the same plus folios and deposits |
| Dashboards and reports | Sales today (by outlet, top items, open tabs, tills, voids), sales by outlet, item, category, staff and hour, payments by method and POS, voids and discounts, stock used against stock sold, tips | the same plus occupancy, arrivals, guest list |
| Night audit | Two checks: every till counted and closed, no open tabs or unsettled orders (or carry them to tomorrow) | plus arrivals, departures and departing balances |
| Setup they start with | Tabs on (limit ₦100,000, flagged after 4 hours), waiters don't take payment, till float ₦10,000, VAT 7.5 % included, opening hours 10:00 to 02:00, receipts only to guests | Standard hotel setup |
| Emails and notices | Welcome, handover pack, day closed, invoices and reminders say "lounge" / "your business"; no rooms, bookings or check-out | as before |
| Guest messages | Receipts sent to the guest on WhatsApp when a payment is taken (charged per message; nothing is printed). No booking confirmations or arrival reminders | plus booking messages and the guest's booking page |

## A business with both (a hotel and a lounge)

Pick **Mixed**. Each location has its own kind. The owner's pages say "locations"; the menu, setup name and help follow the location
chosen in the branch box: room features while the hotel is chosen, none while the lounge is.

## What to say when asked

- **"Do we have to pretend to be a hotel?"** No. Nothing on screen says hotel, room, booking or folio at a lounge, bar or restaurant.
- **"Can we add rooms later?"** Yes. Add rooms to the location and the room pages, stay rules and charge-to-room come back by
  themselves; the settings already made are kept.
- **"Is the price different?"** No. The same share of daily sales, one invoice on the 1st, due in 7 days, nothing debited.
- **"Are SMS charged?"** No. SMS is only for sign-in and phone verification codes. Guest messages are WhatsApp and email.
- **"Can a waiter take money?"** Only if the manager switches on "Waiters take payment" (and "Waiters can take cash" for cash, which
  lands in the cashier's till). By default the cashier or the bar takes payment.
- **"Clubs?"** A club is a kind of location too (same as a lounge on screen).

## Onboarding checklist for a venue

1. Admin → Businesses → **Set up a business for them**: choose the kind (Lounge, Bar, Restaurant, Mixed).
2. Step 4 **Locations**: one row per location with its kind. Step 5 Rooms reads "Not needed".
3. Step 6 **Setup**: the lounge, bar and restaurant setup is applied by itself; add the **outlets** (Bar / lounge, Restaurant, Kitchen).
4. Step 7 People: manager, bar, kitchen, waiters, cashier (no front office or housekeeping offered).
5. Step 8 Connections: POS terminals per outlet desk; WhatsApp number.
6. Go-live checklist (no rooms line), handover pack ("Your business is ready"), the owner accepts the terms themselves.

## Not there yet (say so honestly)

- Table reservations and a floor plan.
- A guest-facing menu or QR ordering.
- Loyalty or membership.
- Scheduled / emailed versions of the outlet reports (they are downloaded as CSV or PDF from Reports).
