# QA script · Hospio360's default colours: indigo on navy (staging)

## Purpose

Hospio360's default look is now **indigo `#4338CA` on deep navy** (it was maroon). Every hotel that hasn't chosen its own
colour shows it. A hotel that set its own colour keeps it. Hotels that had the old maroon saved, which was the old default,
were moved to the new default.

The new default shows in:

- the hotel app: navy sidebar, indigo buttons, links and highlights;
- the admin portal;
- the sign-in screens: the navy panel;
- the guest's booking page and the booking emails;
- the WhatsApp banner;
- the hospio360.com landing site, including its share image and browser-tab icon.

## 1. A hotel on the default

1. Sign in as Tunde (Simpas Ikeja).
   - The sidebar is deep navy.
   - Buttons, links and the selected page are indigo.
   - There's no maroon or pink anywhere: dropdown hovers, the page background, modal backdrops.
2. Hotel setup → **Brand & colours**: the colour shows `#4338CA`, and the first swatch is the indigo.
3. Log out. The sign-in panel is navy, as on the admin sign-in.

## 2. A hotel's own colour still wins

1. Pick teal `#0b4f50` and **Save changes**. The sidebar and buttons turn teal.
2. Log out: the sign-in panel is teal.
3. Put it back: choose the indigo swatch and save. The navy sidebar returns.

## 3. Guests

1. Book a stay for a guest with your email. The email's header and **View booking** button are indigo.
2. The booking page (**View booking**) is indigo.
3. Open the default banner link above. It shows a navy banner with the hotel's name.

## 4. The landing site

1. Open hospio360.com.
   - Its dark sections are navy and its buttons indigo.
   - The "Your brand" section's first example reads "Hospio360 default · Indigo on navy".
2. The browser-tab icon is navy. Share the link in WhatsApp: the preview card is navy.

## What to report

- Any maroon or pink left on a screen, email, banner or the landing site, for a hotel on the default.
- A hotel's own colour replaced by the default.
- Text that's hard to read on the navy or indigo.
